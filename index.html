My name is Jose, and I am a fourth-year student at the University of California, Riverside. I plan to graduate in Spring 2027, and I am excited to see where life takes me after college. Being a university student has helped me become more independent, responsible, and comfortable stepping outside my comfort zone. Throughout my time at UCR, I have learned that growth often comes from trying new things, asking questions, and staying open to different perspectives. I would describe myself as curious, easygoing, and determined. I enjoy learning about the world around me, especially topics involving science, people, and the environment. Although school can be demanding, I try to approach challenges with patience and a sense of humor. I believe that doing your best does not always mean getting everything right immediately. Sometimes, it means being willing to keep going, learn from mistakes, and improve a little each day. That mindset has become an important part of how I approach both school and everyday life.

Outside of academics, I enjoy spending time with friends and family, listening to music, and discovering new places to eat. I like having a balance between being productive and making time for activities that help me relax. Whether I am watching a movie, going on a drive, or having a conversation with someone close to me, I appreciate experiences that make ordinary days more memorable. College has also taught me a lot about managing my time and understanding my priorities. Balancing assignments, exams, and personal responsibilities has not always been easy, but it has helped me develop stronger habits. I have learned to break large tasks into smaller steps and ask for help when I need it. One of my favorite parts of college is meeting people with different backgrounds and experiences. Those interactions remind me that everyone has their own story. They have encouraged me to listen more carefully, communicate clearly, and become more understanding of others. I hope to carry those lessons into future friendships and professional relationships.

As graduation gets closer, I am thinking more seriously about the future I want to build. My goal is to find a career that allows me to keep learning, solve problems, and contribute something meaningful. I am still exploring different possibilities, but I know I want work that challenges me and gives me opportunities to grow. Beyond my career, I hope to travel, experience different cultures, and become more confident navigating unfamiliar situations. I also want to stay connected to the people who have supported me throughout my education. Their encouragement reminds me to appreciate my progress, even when the next step feels uncertain. Ultimately, I want to build a life that reflects my values, interests, and effort. I know my plans may change, and I am comfortable leaving room for new opportunities. For now, I am focused on finishing college, enjoying the experience, and preparing for whatever comes next with curiosity and determination.
